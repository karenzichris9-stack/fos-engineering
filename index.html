About Us

FOS (Factor of Safety) Engineering Ltd is a specialized geotechnical, ground engineering, and construction support company dedicated to delivering innovative, practical, and cost-effective solutions for challenging soil, slope, excavation, and foundation conditions.

As Rwanda continues to experience rapid urbanization, increased land utilization, and infrastructure development, projects are increasingly being developed on steep terrain, unstable slopes, and sites requiring deep excavations.

We combine engineering expertise, practical construction experience, and advanced analytical tools to provide solutions from project conception through design, construction, and completion.

Our Vision

To become the leading geotechnical and ground engineering solutions provider in Rwanda and the East African region by delivering safe, innovative, and sustainable engineering solutions.

Our Mission

To provide professional geotechnical engineering services through comprehensive investigations, advanced analysis, innovative design, and practical construction solutions that ensure the safety, stability, and sustainability of infrastructure developments.

The Challenge We Address

One of the most overlooked aspects of construction projects is the early consideration of geotechnical conditions during project planning and design.

Overlooked Assessments

Architectural and structural designs are often completed without sufficient assessment of slope stability, deep excavation requirements, groundwater conditions, or soil bearing capacity.

Costly Consequences

As a result, projects encounter excavation failures, slope instability, building settlement, construction delays, and emergency stabilization works.

Our Solution

We help clients identify and eliminate these risks before construction begins through early geotechnical involvement integrated into the feasibility and concept design stage.

Supporting Contractors Through Safe Site Preparation and Construction

At FOS Engineering Ltd, we understand that the highest risks often occur during excavation and substructure construction.

We provide practical engineering support to contractors during site preparation, excavation, earthworks, foundation works, and retaining structure construction to ensure safe and efficient execution.

Our objective is to help contractors safely progress from site preparation to completed substructures while minimizing risks, delays, and additional costs.
